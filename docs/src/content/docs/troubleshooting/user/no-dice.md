---
title: No dice, or not all of them
description: The settings that hide the 3D dice of a roll, or some of them.
---

GM settings are in the Dice So Nice! section of **Module Settings** (see [GM Settings](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/getting-started/#gm-settings)). Player settings are in the [Preferences](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/preferences/) tab of the Dice So Nice! dialog.

- **Dice visibility** (player) - "Hide all" shows no dice, "Show only mine" hides the dice of the other players. Also in the [sidebar tab](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/getting-started/#sidebar-tab).
- **Hide 3D dice on GM/Blind rolls** (GM, on by default) - Players who can't see the result of a GM or blind roll see no dice. **Show Ghost dice for hidden rolls** shows them dice without numbers instead.
- **Disable 3D dice on NPC rolls** (GM) - No dice for the rolls of characters no player owns, for the GM too.
- **Disabled for Initiative rolls** and **Disabled during combat** (GM).
- **Disabled for manually entered rolls** (GM, on by default) - No dice when every die of a roll was typed in by hand or read from physical dice.
- **Enable 3D dice on Roll Tables** (GM, off by default) - The roll table must also show its roll formula in the chat.
- **Enable 3D dice on Inline Rolls** (GM, on by default) - For the rolls written in the text of a chat message, like `[[1d20]]`.
- **Max Number of Dice** (GM) - A big roll shows no more dice than this. The result in the chat is still complete.
- **Skip animations when tab is inactive** (player) - When you come back to Foundry from another browser tab, the rolls made in the last 10 seconds are played and the older ones are skipped. With this setting, they are all skipped.
- **Disable Game Canvas** (a Foundry core setting) - Dice So Nice! needs the game canvas and doesn't run without it.
