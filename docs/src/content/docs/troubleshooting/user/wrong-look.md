---
title: The dice don't look like mine
description: The settings that give a roll the dice of another player, a role or a character.
---

GM settings are in the Dice So Nice! section of **Module Settings** (see [GM Settings](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/getting-started/#gm-settings)). Player settings are in the [Preferences](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/preferences/) tab of the Dice So Nice! dialog.

- **Use character owner's dice appearance** (GM, "All rolls" by default) - A roll for a character uses the dice of the player who owns it, even when the GM rolls. "Initiative rolls only" or "Disabled" changes that.
- **Apply optional dice role appearances** (player, on by default) - Dice with a role, like damage types, take the look of that role. Turn it off to see your own dice. Roles your game system marks as essential keep their look.
- **Dice Roles table** (GM) - The GM can give a role its look for the whole table, see [Dice Roles table](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/preferences/#dice-roles-table-gm-only).
- **Per-actor appearance** - A character can have its own dice, see [Per-Actor Appearance](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/actor-appearance/).
