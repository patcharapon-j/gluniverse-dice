---
title: Players see my secret rolls
description: The settings that decide what players see and hear of a GM or blind roll.
---

GM settings are in the Dice So Nice! section of **Module Settings** (see [GM Settings](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/getting-started/#gm-settings)). Player settings are in the [Preferences](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/guide/preferences/) tab of the Dice So Nice! dialog.

- **Hide 3D dice on GM/Blind rolls** (GM, on by default) - When it's off, everyone sees the real dice of GM and blind rolls.
- **Show Ghost dice for hidden rolls** (GM) - Players see dice without numbers: they know a roll happened, not its result. It can show them "To all players", "To the roll author only", or "For player rolls only (hide GM rolls)".
- **Mute all sounds for GM/Blind/Self rolls** (player) - Players won't hear your secret dice through voice chat.

If the settings are right and players still see the dice, the roll comes from your game system or a module: report it to them. Their developers can check [Players see the dice of a secret roll](/foundryvtt-dice-so-nice/troubleshooting/dev/secret-rolls/).
